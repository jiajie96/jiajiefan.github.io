# Jiajie Fan

Founding Engineer at **Kyrall**, building AI-powered 3D CAD generation systems. PhD in Advanced Computer Science from **Leiden University**, sponsored by **BMW AG**.

## Research

My research focuses on deep generative models for engineering design -- bridging machine learning and real-world product design. Topics include spectral-domain diffusion models, NURBS surface learning, and plausibility evaluation of AI-generated designs.

## Publications

- **SpoDify** -- Spectral-domain diffusion modeling for 3D shape generation (IEEE CAI 2026)
- **FDD** -- Plausibility evaluation with denoising autoencoders (ECCV 2024)
- **NeuroNURBS** -- Efficient surface representations for 3D solids (Under Review)
- **PODM** -- Noise scheduling for plausible design generation (arXiv 2023)
- **SA-ALAE** -- Adversarial latent autoencoder for structural image synthesis (IEEE CAI 2024)
- **GeoDiffusion** -- Training-free 3D geometric conditioning in image generation (ICCV 2025)
